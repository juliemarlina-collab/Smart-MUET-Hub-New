01 / CHOOSE A GAME

Your four game folders.

Each game trains one MUET skill. Tap a folder for the moves and a few tricks to score higher.

PINCH · WRITING + SPEAKING

Grab & ThrowPEEL and PREP builder

No score yet↗

Throw each sentence into the Point, Explain, Example or Link bucket to build a paragraph. Speaking rounds use PREP. In Diploma and Exam mode, one sentence is off-topic: bin it.

Tips & tricks
  1. Find the Point first: it is the most general sentence.
  2. The Example names a real person, place or number.
  3. Off-topic sentences sound true but do not support the answer.
  4. Camera mode: pinch to grab, open your fingers to throw.

POINT · LISTENING + READING

Air SliceTrap slicer

No score yet↗

Read the clue, then slice the right answer as the options fly past. Every trap you hit is named: changed plan, word match, too far, condition.

Tips & tricks
  1. Read the question before the options appear.
  2. Watch for “but”, “actually”, “unless” and “provided”: the answer often follows them.
  3. If an option repeats a word from the clue, check it twice.
  4. Camera mode: point your finger and swipe through the answer.

FIST · ALL FOUR PAPERS

Monster PunchVault boss battle

No score yet↗

Punch the monster holding the right answer to beat the boss of each Vault level: Jerebu, Kalut and Gerhana. Below half health the boss enrages and the timer gets shorter.

Tips & tricks
  1. Beat the boss after you finish that Vault level.
  2. Keep your combo going: 3 clean hits = ×2, 6 = ×3.
  3. In Listening questions, tap “Hear it” before you read the options.
  4. Camera mode: hover over a monster, then make a fist.
