Five stages to your band

For a beginner starting from zero. Each stage tells you what to expect, where to get help, what to do and how you know you are improving.

  1. Stage 01 · Week 0

    StartKnow MUET

    Expect
    What MUET is: 4 papers, bands 1 to 5+, and what your target band means.
    Help
    Welcome tour, eBook chapter 1, band and CEFR table.
    Do
    Read eBook pages 1–16. Choose First MUET or Returning. Set your target band.
    Feedback
    Your profile is saved. First XP on the Me page.
    Open Know MUET →
  2. Stage 02 · Day 1

    Check10-minute check

    Expect
    Find your strongest and weakest paper in 10 minutes, with no pressure.
    Help
    One question at a time, a hint on every item, no timer.
    Do
    20 quick items across Listening, Speaking, Reading and Writing.
    Feedback
    A score for each paper, your starting paper and a 3-week plan.
    Take the check →
  3. Stage 03 · Weeks 1–4

    Learn4 skill guides

    Expect
    One strategy per paper and six short steps. You always know the next step.
    Help
    CLUE · PREP · SCAN · PEEL, worked examples, language bank.
    Do
    Try it first, spot the trap, eBook quizzes with stars.
    Feedback
    Auto marking, stars and XP, a 6-step progress bar.
    Open Learn →
  4. Stage 04 · Weeks 3–8

    PractiseLab → Vault → Mock

    Expect
    Real MUET-style papers, from guided to exam level.
    Help
    Lab tips, Vault folders with tips, Arcade levels: Warm-up, Diploma, Exam.
    Do
    Labs, then Vault 1, Vault 2 and Mock 1. Arcade for speed and vocabulary.
    Feedback
    Score /90 per paper, total /360, band and CEFR, Review list of mistakes.
    Open Practice →
  5. Stage 05 · Every week

    TrackMy progress

    Expect
    See your band move and know what to fix next.
    Help
    Results table, error log, lecturer rubric for Speaking and Writing.
    Do
    Reflect on each paper, redo Review items, keep your streak.
    Feedback
    Badges, streak, PDF for your lecturer, and a certificate when all 9 activities are done.
    Open Me →
