05 / CHECK & IMPROVE

Review like an examiner.
Revise one thing.

Reread your draft against the four MUET criteria: task fulfilment, organisation, structure and lexis. Ticks are your own judgement, not an automated mark or band.

SENTENCE UPGRADE

Make one weak sentence stronger.

Example. Basic: “Internships are good. Students learn many things.” Developed: “Internships let students apply classroom theory to real problems, such as using a costing method on an actual project budget.”
