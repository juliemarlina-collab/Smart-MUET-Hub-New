MODEL DIALOGUE / PART 1 STYLE

When will the study group meet?

A  Tuesday morning   B  Wednesday afternoon   C  Thursday afternoon

Answer: C. “That was the plan, but…” cancels Tuesday; “Wednesday’s no good” rejects Wednesday; “Let’s make it then” confirms Thursday.

Optional device narration; the written model is always available.
