ORIGINAL SHORT TEXT / WORKED QUESTION

A food bank that grew

When the student council opened a small food bank in the library foyer last March, organisers expected about twenty visitors a week. Within a month, nearly eighty students were collecting basic items such as rice, oats and canned food. The council responded by moving the food bank to a larger room beside the cafeteria and recruiting volunteers from three different clubs. Not everyone welcomed the change at first. Some students worried that the new location was too public. To protect privacy, volunteers now prepare packs in advance so that visitors can collect them quickly.

Question: Why do volunteers now prepare packs in advance?
A  To serve more students each day
B  To help visitors feel comfortable
C  To reduce the cost of food

Answer: B. “To protect privacy … collect them quickly” means visitors do not have to stay long in a public place.
