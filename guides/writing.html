SECTION 02 / 06

Learn PEEL. Develop, don’t just list.

WHAT TO DO

Select each letter, read the action and try saying the action in your own words. Use the arrow keys to move between letters.

PEEL supports Task 2 body paragraphs. It is not a fixed template for every sentence or for a Task 1 email. For Task 1, respond naturally to every note in an appropriate tone.

TASK 1 / CONNECT WITH THE READER

Read → respond → check.

Identify who wrote to you, the purpose and all the notes. Plan a response to each note. Match the tone to the relationship.

TASK 2 / BUILD AN ARGUMENT

Plan → develop → review.

Choose a clear position that fits the question. Develop your main ideas in connected paragraphs and finish with a conclusion.

SECTION 03 / 06

See an example. Notice the choices.

WHAT TO DO

Choose Task 1 or Task 2. Read the full sample, then open the notes below it. Compare its choices with the purpose of that task.

What makes these models useful? ↗

Task 1: the reply accepts the invitation, suggests when and where to meet, says what to bring and offers help. Its friendly tone suits a classmate.

Task 2: the essay gives a clear position, develops two benefits with examples, considers practical limits and returns to its position in the conclusion.

PEEL example: “Flexible spaces support group work” is the point; the need to discuss and arrange materials explains it; movable tables illustrate it; matching the space to the task links it back.

These are original learning models, not official marked scripts or guaranteed band answers.
