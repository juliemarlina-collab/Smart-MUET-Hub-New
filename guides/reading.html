SECTION 03 / 06

See an example. Follow the evidence.

WHAT TO DO

Read the short text. Ask: “Why did the programme expand?” Make a prediction before revealing the explanation.

ORIGINAL SHORT PRACTICE TEXT

A programme that grew

At the start of the semester, the campus mentoring programme ran one session each week. Demand increased sharply after students shared positive feedback, so the organisers added two extra sessions. This change allowed more students to participate without increasing the size of each group.

The organisers have kept the sessions free. Students still need to register because places in each session are limited. The team will review attendance before deciding whether to expand again.

For Question 4 only:

“The organisers added two extra sessions. [ GAP ] However, students still need to register.”

Reason → result

“Demand increased sharply” explains why the organisers added sessions. “More people wanted to join” is a paraphrase of that reason. The extra sessions are the result.

Watch out: a familiar word does not make an option correct. Confirm the whole meaning.

ORIGINAL SHORT PRACTICE TEXT

A programme that grew

At the start of the semester, the campus mentoring programme ran one session each week. Demand increased sharply after students shared positive feedback, so the organisers added two extra sessions. This change allowed more students to participate without increasing the size of each group.

The organisers have kept the sessions free. Students still need to register because places in each session are limited. The team will review attendance before deciding whether to expand again.

For Question 4 only:

“The organisers added two extra sessions. [ GAP ] However, students still need to register.”

1. Why did the programme add two sessions?
2. What does “this change” refer to?
3. Which conclusion is supported by the text?
4. Choose the sentence that best fills the gap.

SECTION 05 / 06

Check & improve. Name your evidence.

WHAT TO DO

Return to Section 04 to check your answers if you have not done so. Read each explanation there. Then use these checks to diagnose your approach.

01 / PARAPHRASE

Same idea.
Different words.

Write: demand increased = more people wanted to join. Build a small bank of meaning pairs.

02 / REFERENCE

Look back.
Read forward.

“This change” refers to the added sessions. For gaps, check pronouns, linkers and the logic on both sides.

03 / INFERENCE

Stay within
the evidence.

A reasonable inference is supported by the passage. Do not invent a guarantee such as “everyone gets a place”.
