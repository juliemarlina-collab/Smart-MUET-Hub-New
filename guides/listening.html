SECTION 03 / 06

See an example. Notice the contrast.

WHAT TO DO

Read this mini-dialogue first. Decide which option the speaker prefers, then reveal the explanation. This written demonstration prepares you for the audio activity.

SPEAKER 1

“The first bag is cheaper. Is that the one you would buy?”

SPEAKER 2

“It is cheaper, but I would still choose the second one because it lasts longer.”

Reveal the CLUE thinking ↗

Check: the question asks for the final preference.

Listen: “but” and “still choose” signal the key meaning.

Understand: durability matters more than the lower price here.

Eliminate: reject the first bag; being cheaper does not make it the preferred choice.

SECTION 05 / 06

Check & improve. Replay with a focus.

WHAT TO DO

Read the feedback in Section 04 first. Replay the track for a detail you missed, then explain the clue in your own words.

01 / PURPOSE

Catch
the idea.

For the first question, focus on Leanne’s role in the club, not simply on the mention of a pet.

02 / CAUSE

Hear
the reason.

Distinguish who built the house from why it was easy to build. A person and a reason answer different questions.

03 / ATTITUDE

Notice
the reaction.

For Amir’s surprise, listen to what he reacts to. Do not answer using general knowledge about tortoises.

Evidence summary after your attempt ↗

Leanne is the Pet Club president. The house has a simple design. Amir reacts to tortoises being sensitive to touch. These are summaries of the supplied practice explanations, not a full transcript.
