00 / START HERE · KNOW MUET

A clear start. A stronger plan.

MUET is the Malaysian University English Test, set by Majlis Peperiksaan Malaysia (MPM). It shows how well you use English for study in higher education. Know the four papers first, then learn one technique for each.

4papers
90marks each
360total score
5 yrsresult validity
800/1ListeningCLUEAbout 50 min · 5 parts · 30 questions. Every recording is played twice.Learn CLUE → 800/2SpeakingPREPAbout 30 min per group · Part 1 a 2-minute talk · Part 2 a group discussion.Learn PREP → 800/3ReadingSCAN75 min · 7 parts · 40 questions. Short texts, long articles and a gapped text.Learn SCAN → 800/4WritingPEEL75 min · Task 1 an email reply (100+ words) · Task 2 an essay (250+ words).Learn PEEL →

Speaking is taken on its own day. Reading, Writing and Listening are on the written-test day. Always confirm dates and rules on the MPM portal ↗. More in the eBook, Chapter 1 and Chapter 2.
